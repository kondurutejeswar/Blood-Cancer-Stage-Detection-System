Classification Stages

Real-world leukemia progression stages detected by our AI model

✅

Benign (Stage 0)

Normal blood morphology - No leukemic cells detected. Lymphocytes within normal range (4,000-10,000/μL). No organ enlargement or symptoms present. 95% of healthy population.

No Risk
🔵

Early Stage (Rai Stage I)

Initial lymphocyte elevation (15,000-30,000/μL) with enlarged lymph nodes but no anemia. Patient may have mild fatigue. 5-year survival: 90%+ with monitoring.

Low Risk
⚠️

Pre-Cancerous (Rai Stage II-III)

Moderate lymphocyte proliferation (30,000-100,000/μL) with anemia (hemoglobin <10g /dL). Spleen/liver enlargement common. Progression risk: 20-30% per year.

High Risk
🔴

Pro-Cancerous (Rai Stage IV)

Advanced leukemia (>100,000/μL lymphocytes) with severe anemia & thrombocytopenia ( <50,000 platelets). Multiple organ involvement. Urgent chemotherapy required.

Critical
